Our Services

One team coordinating your whole job.

From a stack of business cards to a trade-show booth, we use the right process for the job — in-house or through trusted trade partners — and tell you honestly which one that is.

Get a quote Call (503) 222-2942 sendjob@citygi.com

  1. 01

    Digital color printing

    Quick-turnaround, short-run, full-color work on our Digital presses — standard sheets up to 13×19½″, and long-sheet printing up to 51 inches for banners, book covers, and panoramic pieces. Business cards, postcards, flyers, brochures, marketing collateral, menus, stationery, variable-data printing.

  2. 02

    Wide format

    Printing up to 50+″ wide — posters, banners, signage, canvas and fine-art prints, with optional mounting and laminating. Trade show panels and banner stands. Larger rigid work (up to 4×8 sheets, routing and cutting) handled through our trusted production partners.

  3. 03

    Design & prepress

    In-house graphic design, file setup and conversion, color correction, proofs, imposition & custom work/projects — tell us what you need.

  4. 04

    Bindery & finishing

    Cutting, folding, saddle stitch, coil binding, perfect binding, Wire-O, laminating, scoring, padding, drilling, collating, shrink wrap, die cutting, round cornering, numbering, perforating, tabbing, mounting on gator or foamcore.

  5. 05

    Mailing services

    List processing, addressing, presorting, and machine inserting — we can take your mailing from design to the post office in one shop.

Promotional products available — call us.
